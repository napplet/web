Showcase credits

Selected releases, reviewed 2026-10-28

Impossible Machine, Rail Game and Sketch Loop preserve their original historical
manifests and bytes. They are explicitly labeled archived releases. Their private
archive adapter is not a current-protocol validator. Stick Brawl, Freeze Frame
Response and Poly Dogfight use signed, direct artifact-hash releases. The player
verifies each selected signature and its artifact's original bytes, then runs it
without shell services. It does not certify full manifest or NAP conformance.

Some public publishers use nostr:// source locations not defined by the current
NIP-5D Source section. We preserve signed events unchanged, flag the discrepancy,
and link only to their HTTPS source archives. The discovered collection is not
a conformance certification. Living specification:
https://github.com/nostr-protocol/nips/pull/2303

Loader adapted from https://github.com/zeSchlausKwab/napplet-soy (packages/runtime/src/index.ts).
